    Space has a power problem

    Every satellite is its own power station, like factories before the power grid.

    The grid changed industry. It’s time to build one in orbit.

    Power through night

    Every satellite spends up to 40% of its orbit in shadow. Beamed power keeps it at full power, day or night.

    Power lower orbits

    Smaller solar arrays mean less drag. Satellites fly lower, for longer.

    Power abundance, not power budgets

    Computing, manufacturing, and telecoms are moving to space.

    This is the space industrial revolution.
