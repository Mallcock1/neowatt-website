Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Who we are

NEOWATT Ltd (company number SC866522, registered in Scotland) operates neowatt.co.uk and is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us at contact@neowatt.co.uk.

What we collect and why

Newsletter sign-up. If you enter your email address in the sign-up form, we store it and use it to send you occasional updates about NEOWATT. We do this on the basis of your consent, which you give by submitting the form. You can withdraw consent at any time (see below).

Email correspondence. If you email us, we keep your message and address for as long as needed to reply and follow up. We do this on the basis of our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries.

Technical data. Like any website, our hosting and font providers record standard technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) in their server logs when you load a page. We do not use this to identify you. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers, and the site does not set cookies.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for their own marketing. We use the following service providers to run the site and the newsletter, and they process data on our behalf:

  • Google (Google Workspace) – stores newsletter sign-ups and sends our emails; also serves the fonts used on this site.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) – hosts the website.

These providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, transfers are covered by UK adequacy regulations or the providers' standard data protection clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address on our newsletter list until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove it. Correspondence is kept for as long as it is relevant to the enquiry.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • object to or restrict how we use it;
  • withdraw your consent to the newsletter at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, or to unsubscribe, email contact@neowatt.co.uk. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use personal data we will update this page and the date at the top.
